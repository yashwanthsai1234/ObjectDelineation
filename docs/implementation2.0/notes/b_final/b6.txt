### B.6.2 Run gates

Common rules for Gates B1 and B3:
- **Value.** Every run gate uses the per-item mean of IoU_tc over the K = 8 answers (`sel_mean_of_k`), whatever
  Gate A picks. The Gate A answer-choice method is reported as a second line. A miss uses the K-mean rule.
- **Pairing and CI.** Paired on the same items. The CI is the Phase A1 **patient-level** bootstrap (resample
  `group_id`, all items of a patient move together) of the per-item difference, 95 %. The gate decision uses the
  BCa interval; the percentile interval is reported beside it, with the cluster count. Reason: nodules of one patient
  share the reading sessions, the scanner and the reconstruction, and on R5 val-select the patient CI is 23 % wider
  than the nodule CI (0.085 vs 0.069) [measured: docs/implementation2.0/reviews/final_r1_data.md, R5 bootstrap].
- **Noise floor.** These CIs cover data variance only, not training-seed variance. Phase A3 evaluates R5 on val
  twice with two sampling seeds; the paired difference is the sampling-noise floor. A point gain must exceed it.
- "Pass" needs every point rule and every CI rule. Rules are fixed before the run (B1.9 prices them). No near-miss is
  accepted after the run.
- R5's per-item values come from `runs/r5/eval/eval_val/items.jsonl` (1,084 items) [measured].
- **Limitations** (also in DOC11): the test split informed the diagnosis (R5's per-offset test results, B.1 item 2);
  from 2026-11-08 every design number uses train or val only. The target law is a synthetic shift law; the guard
  views check other laws.

- **Gate B1** (the whole val: 78 patients, 200 nodules, 1,084 items; baselines in section 1):
  - val IoU: CI lower bound of (B1 - R5) > 0 (R5 0.618). The target 0.66 is reported, not gated. Decomposition: a
    25 % cut of far misses turns about 0.25 x 0.273 x 406 = 28 misses into found images; at the found IoU 0.706 that
    adds about 28 x 0.706 / 1,084 = +0.018 [estimate: arithmetic on measured values]. Reaching 0.66 needs gains on
    found images too.
  - val misses at offset > 0.3: point <= 20.5 % (25 % below R5's 27.3 %) and CI upper bound of (B1 - R5) < 0;
  - val IoU at offset < 0.15: CI lower bound of (B1 - R5) > -0.02 (R5 0.751; no regression on centred images);
  - guard law: val IoU on the `edge` view: CI lower bound of (B1 - R5) > -0.02 (R5 value from A3; B.8.4);
  - legal share on val >= 0.99 (R5 0.995);
  - report only: paired patient-CI difference vs R5 on `lidc-val-rescaled` and `lidc-val-rotated`, with a
    non-inferiority note at -0.02 (when the ledger allows, B.8.1);
  - report only, the extra-steps control: the near-centre gain (offset < 0.15) beside the far gain (a data effect
    shows as a far gain much larger than the near gain), and the slope of R5's held-out IoU over the last 300 of its
    770 steps (`runs/rl/train_rl/rl.json` eval series) beside the B1 result;
  - test is not read here (test once, final candidate only).
- **Gate B2** (preset for B3; B2.4):
  - B1 run: NaN/inf count = 0 over all steps; the counterfactual stats are present for steps 50-300;
  - the chosen preset has ESS >= the control's (median over B1 steps 50-300); else B3 keeps the control;
  - optional fp16 arm (steps 41-60): NaN/inf = 0; the vLLM dtype assertion passed; the paired prompt bootstrap
    95 % lower bound of (fp16 - B1) `rewards/iou_tc` > -0.02 [estimate threshold].
- **Gate B3:**
  - before the run: T1-T12 and T14 pass (`B.spec.md`); lab round (B3.7, val-shape): location Spearman mean > 0.3
    under v5, CI lower bound > 0, and higher than v4 paired by group; drop share v5 <= v4; miss-beats-hit share
    <= 1 % (after `area_gate` if needed);
  - after the run, on val-select (re-dealt halves, B1.0), paired against the B1 run: miss share falls by >= 25 %
    relative (point) and the CI upper bound of (B3 - B1) miss share < 0; IoU on images found by **both** runs: CI
    lower bound of (B3 - B1) > -0.01 (the paired set avoids the composition bias of new, harder hits); `edge` view
    IoU: CI lower bound of (B3 - B1) > -0.02; legal share >= 0.99.

## B.7. Risks and rollback

| Risk | Sign | Mitigation / rollback |
|---|---|---|
| Val shapes the training mix and also judges the gates | val gains more than test | Fixed: the target is the train shifted law (B1.4). No val data reaches training. Test is read once. |
| The model fits the one synthetic shift law of the test view | shifted val gains, `edge` / rescaled / rotated views fall | Guard views (B1.6) with a hard floor on `edge` (Gate B1). |
| Small gate sets (78 patients for B1, 38 for B3) | wide patient CIs; a real gain fails | B1.9 power check before the rental; paired comparisons; fixed rules, no written near-miss acceptance. |
| Gains are seed noise | point gain below the sampling-noise floor | A3 two-seed floor (B.6.2); CIs state that they exclude training-seed variance. |
| Gain comes from 300 more steps, not data | near-centre gain as large as far gain | Near-centre control and R5 plateau slope reported beside Gate B1; ADR-0014 states the confound. |
| `draws` needed for DG7 is large; disk grows | B1.4 table | Only scheduled windows are materialized, so disk stays near 1.2-1.6 GB [estimate from 7,341 windows = 1.2 GB]. |
| A budget cut trains a prefix that misses the target | DG7-DG9 fail on a prefix | 20-step blocks; only whole blocks are trained (B1.2, B1.5). |
| Noisy doctor references give a near-random reward | DG14b share of low agreement | Report before B1.4; owner rule B.8.3 item 4. |
| Rescaled share hurts the shifted test view (Q5 in B1.md) | per-kind logs: rescaled reward rises, shifted flat | Shares are config; set rescaled 0 in a rebuild ($0). |
| fp16 overflow (no VLM in the paper; known Qwen2.5-VL case) | inf/NaN or large activations in the Mac check | Full-model Mac check before the optional arm; on failure the arm is dropped. |
| New private TRL patch (`LLM` dtype) breaks on a TRL update or silently does nothing | A7 start-up check fails; dtype assertion fails | TRL stays pinned 1.17.1; both checks stop the run with a message. Only needed for the optional fp16 arm. |
| B3 preset differs from B1's | B2.4 picks B or E | ADR-0012/0013 note it; the counterfactual stats show the size of the weight change. |
| token_truncate is biased for long answers (secondary sources) | `cf/tok_clamp2_share` high | Option E (OPSM) is the length-fair option; D only under its trigger. |
| KL spike (398,284 once) | `kl` max in log | It left grad_norm at 0.0041 (median 0.0048); a diagnostic, not a driver. |
| Location term gamed | T1-T14; `loc` rises while IoU stays flat | Gates in B3.2; LocGuard (B3.5b) stops the run on two windows of clear evidence. |
| Location kernel too short for real misses | B3.7 d / r median > 3 | `loc_width` or the heavy-tailed kernel, chosen from data. |
| KL balance differs between v4 and v5 | B3.7 median |A| ratio r | `beta = 1e-3 / r` in the B3 config (or 0.0 by owner decision). Adam needs no scale constant. |
| Runaway answers get no gradient | B3.7 rule 9 share | Option O with Phase C's C7 limit (owner decision). |
| c_topo too large: topology dominates IoU | adv sd from topology >> from IoU in lab round | B3.7 rule sets the smallest safe c. |
| Fingerprint split makes all caches stale | `StaleCacheError` | Done once in B1.0, before any build; the error names the file. |
| Kernel change alters old terms | golden test | Outputs are additive; revert the commit if goldens change. |
| Rows dropped, reordered or a different upload loaded | manifest hash, count or ids hash assertion | `train_rl` refuses to start (B1.5). |
| Resume replays a different order | `ids_sha256` / step index | Order is the file; HF resume skips consumed batches; resume test on a 4-step stub (B1.5). |
| Budget overrun | ledger; BudgetStop | B.8 plan; preflight refuses over cap; BudgetStop saves and stops at the cap. |

Rollback is per task: an old prepared root still loads until B1.8 (no `schedule` = old path); numerics are a preset;
reward v4 stays until gate B3; the kernel change is one commit.
