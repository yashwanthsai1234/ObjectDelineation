#### B3.7 Lab round (offline, $0)

- Script `experiments/phase2/reward_lab_v5.py --answers <A3 saved eval answers, K = 8 per item> --view
  data/eval_views/lidc-val-shifted --half shape`. Val-shape only, so val-select stays clean for Gate B3.
- Needs saved answers with text (Phase A3: final_eval saves every answer). Re-score them on the Mac (CPU build of
  gpucheck) and compute v4 and v5 advantages per group.
- Report and rules:
  1. **Location signal in all-miss groups** (a group whose 8 answers are all missed answers). For each all-miss
     group with >= 3 distinct values of d, compute the Spearman correlation of A_i and -d_i / r over its 8 answers,
     where d = centroid distance and r = sqrt(radius2). The value is the mean over groups, with a paired patient
     (group_id) bootstrap 95 % CI (A1). Groups with constant A count as 0 (not excluded), so a rule that zeroes
     groups cannot raise the mean. Gate: v5 mean > 0.3, its CI lower bound > 0, and (v5 - v4) mean > 0 paired by
     group [estimate thresholds]. This replaces v1's "share of all-miss groups with non-zero advantage", which v4
     already has through g_area and F5 (section 1 item 6).
  2. **Drop share:** share of all-miss groups dropped (A = 0 for the whole group), v4 vs v5. Gate: v5 <= v4.
  3. **Variance share:** share of the variance of A in all-miss groups from `w_area * g_area` versus `alpha_loc *
     loc`. If the area term dominates, the location signal is weak; report it.
  4. **Miss beats hit:** share of groups where a missed answer has a higher R than a hit in the same group. If > 1 %,
     set `area_gate = True` (the area term then scales by `min(1, iou_tc / miss_iou)`) and re-measure.
  5. **Distance law:** distribution of d / r for missed answers. If its median is > 3 (the Gaussian kernel is almost
     0 there), set `loc_width` so that the median missed answer gets a location value of at least 0.1, or switch to
     the heavy-tailed kernel; record the choice in ADR-0013. Option to check first: the scorer already sums the
     exact distance to the doctors' union of every spill pixel (`spill`, `spill_n` in `ScoreRows` [code:
     cuda/gpucheck.cpp:1430-1440]), and the distance table is a full-frame EDT [code:
     src/objdel/reward/cache.py:130-135]. So the mean spill distance is available with no new row partial. If it
     predicts IoU gain as well as the centroid term on val-shape, it can replace B3.1 (fewer kernel changes); record
     the comparison.
  6. **Advantage size and KL balance:** median |A| for v4 and for v5 on non-dropped groups; r = median |A_v4| /
     median |A_v5|; the implied effective beta of v5 at beta 1e-3 is 1e-3 x r. The B3 config sets `beta = 1e-3 / r`
     unless the owner chose 0.0 (B.8.3 item 5). A config value, not a reward constant. Record it in ADR-0013.
  7. **Agreement on hits:** Spearman of A_v4 and A_v5 on groups with all IoU >= 0.1; advantage sd per cell.
  8. **Topology fallback:** margin q95 of (IoU vs vote - best same-signature IoU) over legal answers whose signature
     no doctor drew. Set `c_topo` by rule: the smallest c with `c * eps >= that q95` (so the fallback never beats a
     valid topology, the H1 hack in docs/implementation2.0/notes/H2_rl_math.md s3.4).
  9. **Runaway answers:** share of answers that hit the length limit, and their mean v5 R. `mask_truncated_completions`
     is True [code: src/objdel/train/configs.py:188], so a runaway gets no direct negative gradient. Option O (owner
     decision B.8.3 item 7, decided together with Phase C's C7 limit): add DAPO's soft overlong penalty as a term of R
     (weight 1, before the group baseline), using TRL's formula `get_soft_overlong_punishment(max_completion_len=L,
     soft_punish_cache=200)` [code: .venv trl/rewards/other_rewards.py:83]. Not as a separate TRL reward: TRL would
     add it after our group-centred aggregator. Default: off, so the B3 A/B changes only the reward of v5.
- Write all values and the evidence into ADR-0013.
