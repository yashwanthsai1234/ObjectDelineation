## 1. MEDIUM: illegal answers counted toward the Phase 2 geometry gates (review #2)

**Decision (coordinator).** In eval, an illegal answer scores 0 on IoU_tc, F5, g_area and the Dice-type scores,
which is how the reward treats it: `advantages_v4` zeroes every term of an illegal rollout before the z-score.

**One rule.** `objdel.reward.legal_gate(T) = T["legal"] >= 0.5` is the gate. `advantages_v4` (`X = R * legal_gate(T)`),
`topology_valid` and `eval.metrics` all call it. The goldens show that the advantages and r_topo are unchanged.

**Fix (`eval/metrics.py`).**
- Per sample: `iou_tc`, `f5`, `g_area` are legality-gated (`T[t] * legal`). These are the same arrays the GRPO
  signal check already used. `advantage` and `passed` still come from the raw terms, because the reward gates them
  itself.
- Per object: the `_ZEROED` scores (IoU_tc, F5, g_area, IoU / Dice vs vote, D_max) are zeroed for `~legal`
  instead of `~format_ok`. Legal implies parsed.
- The soft-Dice probability map and GED use `drawn = masks & legal`, so an illegal raster is empty, like a failed
  one.
- Areas, area ratios and components keep the parsed raster, so the hack audit still measures what an illegal output
  drew. RHR now counts it as a failure.
- Docstrings: the metrics module now states that `iou_tc_pass` is the geometric part of the pass criterion (legal
  and IoU_tc >= 0.5 for every object), with topology gated separately. The gate `REQUIREMENTS` text reads
  "median IoU_tc (illegal = 0)" and "rollouts legal with IoU_tc >= 0.5 (every object)".

**Tests (red, then green).**
- `test_eval.py::test_illegal_answers_score_zero_on_geometry` is the reviewer's repro: 8 copies of a square with a
  collinear spike against two near-identical raters. Before the fix it failed on `samples.iou_tc == 1.0`. After the
  fix, legal = pass = 0, every geometric score is 0, `soft_dice` = 0, and the area ratio is 400 / 420 (the audit
  still sees the raster). `summarize` gives `iou_tc_pass` = 0 and IoU_tc / F5 medians of 0, `phase2_entry` fails
  `geometry_pass` and `geometry_median`, and audit RHR = 1.
- `test_reward.py::test_legal_gate_is_the_one_legality_rule`: the gate's values, `topology_valid` false on illegal
  rollouts, and `advantages_v4` unchanged when the illegal rollouts' terms are randomised.
- The existing gate, audit and alarm tests (`test_eval.py`, `test_eval_data.py`, `test_breakit_*`) pass unchanged.

is the per-structure union rule plus `"rim"` for RIGA.
