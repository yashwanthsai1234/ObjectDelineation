## Research and sources

- Bit quads: S. B. Gray, "Local properties of binary images in two dimensions", IEEE Trans. Computers C-20(5):551–561,
  1971, https://doi.org/10.1109/T-C.1971.223289 (Crossref: https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1109/T-C.1971.223289).
  Pratt, *Digital Image Processing* 3rd ed. pp. 593–595, the reference of Octave's bweuler:
  https://octave.sourceforge.io/image/function/bweuler.html. Yao et al., SpringerPlus 4:735, 2015, which restates Gray's
  algorithm: https://doi.org/10.1186/s40064-015-1511-8.
- Polygonize:
  - cracks as 1-cells: Kovalevsky, CVGIP 46(2):141–161, 1989, https://doi.org/10.1016/0734-189X(89)90165-5;
  - the (8, 4) pairing: Kong–Rosenfeld, CVGIP 48(3):357–393, 1989, https://doi.org/10.1016/0734-189X(89)90147-3;
  - saddle disambiguation: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marching_squares;
  - list ranking: Wyllie, Cornell TR79-387, 1979, https://hdl.handle.net/1813/7502;
  - min-label pointer jumping: Shiloach–Vishkin, J. Algorithms 3(1):57–67, 1982,
    https://doi.org/10.1016/0196-6774(82)90008-6, and Hillis–Steele, CACM 29(12):1170–1183, 1986,
    https://doi.org/10.1145/7902.7903;
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pointer_jumping and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shoelace_formula.
  - Suzuki–Abe is not cited, because the code does not trace contours sequentially.
  - While writing the polygonize paragraph I checked that a ring through a saddle vertex visits that vertex twice
    (two diagonal pixels: the outer ring passes (1, 1) going east and then going west). So the docstring says
    "the ring touches itself at that vertex" and does not call it a simple curve.
- certify: Gray 1971 and Kong–Rosenfeld 1989 for chi8 = b0 − b1. The rest points to the three modules' lists.
- census_sizerule: plan §4.2, M3 note, the Phase 0 census scripts, Vincent 1994 (area opening / closing,
  https://www.doi.org/10.1007/978-3-662-03039-4_13), Kong–Rosenfeld 1989.
- build_targets: plan §4.4 / §7, M5 note, `objdel.targets` for the algorithms, transformers AutoTokenizer and tokenizer
  `__call__` docs, `multiprocessing.Pool.imap`.
